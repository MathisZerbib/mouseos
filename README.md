| | |
|---|---|
| **Computer** | Trackpad, air mouse, keyboard, volume, music, your apps in one tap, see the screen on your phone. |
| **TV** | Samsung, LG and Google TV remote, keyboard included. |
| **Slides** | Next, previous, timer. |

```sh
brew tap mathiszerbib/mouseos https://github.com/MathisZerbib/mouseos
brew install --cask mouseos
```

## Three steps

1. **Install MouseOS on your computer** and open it.
2. **Open MouseOS on your phone**, on the same Wi‑Fi, and sign in.
3. **Click ALLOW on the computer.** Your phone is the mouse.

## Private

Phone and computer talk directly over your Wi‑Fi. No tracking. Every new phone needs one ALLOW click on the computer. [Privacy policy](https://mouseos.app/privacy-policy.html) · [Terms](https://mouseos.app/terms.html)

## Stuck?

- **Mac won't open it** → System Settings → Privacy & Security → **Open Anyway**.
- **Windows protected your PC** → **More info → Run anyway**.
- **Phone doesn't find the computer** → same Wi‑Fi, VPN off, or turn on your phone's hotspot.
- **Anything else** → [support@mouseos.app](mailto:support@mouseos.app) · [FAQ](https://mouseos.app/#faq)
