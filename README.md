## Private by design

- **No account, no cloud, no tracking.** Phone and Mac talk directly over your Wi‑Fi.
- **Nobody else takes over your Mac.** Every new phone needs one ALLOW click on the Mac.
- [Privacy policy](https://mathiszerbib.github.io/mouseos/privacy-policy.html)

## Stuck?

- **macOS won't open it** — System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, **Open Anyway**. Only the first time: MouseOS isn't notarized yet.
- **The phone doesn't find the Mac** — same Wi‑Fi, VPN off. Café or hotel Wi‑Fi hides devices from each other: turn on your phone's hotspot and join it from the Mac, or tap **TYPE THE ADDRESS** on the phone.
- **The pointer doesn't move** — System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility: switch MouseOS on.
