Install

Two minutes. Once.

Start on your Mac, finish on your phone. Keep both on the same Wi‑Fi.

  1. On your Mac

    Download and open

    Drag MouseOS into Applications and open it. If macOS stops it the first time: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.

    Download for Mac Apple silicon · macOS 11+

  2. On your Mac

    Allow control

    Click ALLOW CONTROL, then switch MouseOS on. macOS asks once before any app can move the pointer.

  3. On your phone

    Open the phone app

    Install MouseOS on your Android phone and open it on the same Wi‑Fi. It finds your Mac by itself.

    Google Play Android 8+

  4. Back on your Mac

    Click ALLOW

    Your Mac asks once for each new phone. That's it — your phone is the mouse.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes — phone app and Mac app.

Which Macs?

Apple silicon (M1 and later), macOS 11 or later.

iPhone? Windows?

Android first. iPhone is on the way.

Which TVs?

Samsung, LG and Google TV (Sony, TCL, Philips…).

Can someone else take over my Mac?

No. Every new phone needs one ALLOW click on the Mac.

Stuck?

macOS won't open it

System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, click Open Anyway. Only the first time.

The phone doesn't find the Mac

Same Wi‑Fi, VPN off. Café or hotel Wi‑Fi hides devices: turn on your phone's hotspot and join it from the Mac. Or tap TYPE THE ADDRESS on the phone.

The pointer doesn't move

System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility: switch MouseOS on. After an update, the Mac app shows you how.
