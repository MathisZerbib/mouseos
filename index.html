Install

Two minutes. Once.

Start on your computer, finish on your phone. Keep both on the same Wi‑Fi.

  1. On your Mac

    Download and open

    Drag MouseOS into Applications and open it. If macOS stops it the first time: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.

    Download for Mac Apple silicon · macOS 11+

  2. On your Mac

    Allow control

    Click ALLOW CONTROL, then switch MouseOS on. macOS asks once before any app can move the pointer.

  3. On your phone

    Open the phone app

    Install MouseOS on your Android phone and open it on the same Wi‑Fi. It finds your Mac by itself.

    Google Play Android 8+

  4. Back on your Mac

    Click ALLOW

    Your Mac asks once for each new phone. That's it — your phone is the mouse.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes — the phone app and the computer app.

Which computers?

Mac with Apple silicon (M1 and later), macOS 11 or later. Windows 10 and 11, 64‑bit. Linux, 64‑bit — Ubuntu, Debian, Fedora and friends, on X11 or Wayland.

iPhone?

Android first. iPhone is on the way.

Which TVs?

Samsung, LG and Google TV (Sony, TCL, Philips…).

Can someone else take over my computer?

No. Every new phone needs one ALLOW click on the computer.

Stuck?

macOS won't open it

System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, click Open Anyway. Only the first time.

Windows says it protected your PC

Click More info, then Run anyway. Only the first time: MouseOS isn't signed by Microsoft yet.

The phone doesn't find the computer

Same Wi‑Fi, VPN off. Café or hotel Wi‑Fi hides devices: turn on your phone's hotspot and join it from the computer. On Windows, your Wi‑Fi must be a Private network. Or tap TYPE THE ADDRESS on the phone.

The pointer doesn't move

Mac: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility: switch MouseOS on. Linux on Wayland: open MouseOS and click ALLOW CONTROL. After an update, the app shows you how.
