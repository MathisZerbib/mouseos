Install

Set up in a minute.

  1. 01

    Get the Mac app

    Download it, drag it to Applications, open it.

    Download for Mac

  2. 02

    Allow control

    Click ALLOW CONTROL, then flip the MouseOS switch.

  3. 03

    Open the phone app

    Same Wi‑Fi. Click ALLOW on the Mac. Done.

    Google Play soon
macOS won't open the app

System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, Open Anyway. Once.

The phone doesn't find the Mac

Same Wi‑Fi, VPN off. On café or hotel Wi‑Fi, turn on your phone's hotspot and join it from the Mac. Or tap TYPE THE ADDRESS on the phone.

The pointer doesn't move

System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility: turn MouseOS on.

Questions

Android first. iPhone is on the way.

Which TVs?

Samsung, LG and Google TV (Sony, TCL, Philips…).

No. Every new phone needs one ALLOW click on the Mac.
