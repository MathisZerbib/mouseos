MouseOS — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

MouseOS (the Android app "MouseOS — Mac & TV Remote" and the macOS companion "MouseOS for Mac") is a remote control for your own computer and TV. It is built so that no personal data ever leaves your local network.

What the app does with data

  • No account, no sign-in, no cloud. The app never contacts a server operated by us or by anyone else. There is no analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDK.
  • Local network only. Pointer movements, key presses, media commands and TV commands are sent directly from your phone to your Mac or TV over your Wi-Fi. They are not stored.
  • Stored on your phone only: your preferences (theme, pointer speed, last screen), the address and name of the last Mac you linked, your quick-launch shortcuts, the pairing tokens your TV issued to this phone, and a random identifier the app generates so that your Mac can remember this phone after you click ALLOW once. This identifier is shown to your Mac only; it is not linked to you and never sent elsewhere. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.
  • Stored on your Mac only: the list of phones you allowed.

Permissions

  • Network / Wi-Fi state / multicast: to discover your Mac and TV on the local network and talk to them.
  • High sampling rate sensors: the gyroscope drives the air pointer. Sensor readings are turned into pointer movement on your phone; raw sensor data is never stored or transmitted.
  • Vibrate: the talk timer buzzes at 5 min, 1 min and time-up.

The app requests no location, contacts, camera, microphone, storage or SMS permission.

Children

MouseOS is a general-purpose utility, not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published at the same address and the date above is updated.
